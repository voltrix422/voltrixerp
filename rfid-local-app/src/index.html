Voltrix RFID Local

Connect to your reader over the network, then scan tags and sync to Voltrix ERP.

How to connect

  1. Set Subnet (e.g. 192.168.18) or type the reader’s full Reader IP.
  2. Click Search Readers, then click a reader in the list (port updates automatically).
  3. Click Connect. When status shows Connected or Scanning, use Start Scan / Stop Scan if needed.
  4. Use Disconnect when you want to stop (closes listen mode or the reader session).
